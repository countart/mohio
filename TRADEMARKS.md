## 3. General Rule

You may truthfully refer to MOHIO in plain text to identify that your application, project, article, tutorial, integration, or service works with or was built using MOHIO, provided that your use is accurate, non-misleading, and does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, certification, affiliation, official status, compatibility approval, or support by Particular LLC.

You may not use the MOHIO Marks in a way that suggests your product, service, fork, runtime, compiler, platform, profile, langmap, connector, development environment, marketplace, cloud service, compliance tool, audit service, training program, certification program, or enterprise offering is official, certified, endorsed, sponsored, operated, supported, or provided by Particular LLC unless Particular LLC has given you express written permission.

## 4. Permitted Uses Without Written Permission

Subject to the restrictions in this policy, you may use the word "Mohio" in plain text to make truthful statements such as:

- "This app was built with Mohio."
- "This project uses the Mohio public core."
- "This package integrates with Mohio."
- "This tutorial explains how to write a basic Mohio program."
- "This library provides an unofficial connector for Mohio."
- "This application requires a compatible Mohio runtime."
- "This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Particular LLC."

You may use MOHIO Marks in text for news reporting, commentary, comparison, criticism, education, documentation, technical discussion, or compatibility descriptions, provided that the use is truthful and does not create confusion about source, sponsorship, certification, compatibility approval, support, or affiliation.

## 5. Required Attribution

When reasonably practical, third-party projects that refer to MOHIO should include the following attribution:

> MOHIO is a trademark of Particular LLC. This project is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, certified by, or supported by Particular LLC.

For user-facing products, services, documentation, websites, repositories, package listings, app listings, marketplace listings, and commercial materials, this attribution should be reasonably visible.
